The pipeline

You write the rules. xark does the proof.

Mark inputs public or private in Noir, assert what must hold. xark lowers the circuit to Groth16 over BN254 and generates a Solana verifier that checks it through the native alt_bn128 syscalls.

Noir→ ACIR + witness→ Groth16 · BN254→ alt_bn128→ Solana program
